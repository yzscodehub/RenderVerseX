Modifications
=============

The vendored ZIP and extracted source bytes are unmodified. RenderVerseX
converts the OBJ/MTL source to glTF 2.0 using
Scripts/Assets/convert_crytek_sponza.py. The conversion triangulates OBJ
polygons, normalizes texture paths, converts Phong material factors to a
deterministic metallic-roughness approximation, preserves diffuse textures,
and places the source centimeter-scale scene under a 0.01 root scale.

The attribution and license in the original archive's copyright.txt are also
retained beside the source data.
